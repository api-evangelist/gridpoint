Most multi-site organizations can tell you their total energy spend to the dollar. Far fewer can tell you which sites are efficient, why costs vary across the portfolio, or what next quarter's energy line will be within a tight range. That gap — between knowing the total and understanding the components — is both an accountability problem and a forecasting problem.
