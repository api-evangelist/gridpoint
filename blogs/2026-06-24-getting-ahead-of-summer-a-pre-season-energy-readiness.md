The cheapest time to deal with summer energy costs is before summer starts. Once temperatures climb and equipment is running flat out, your options narrow to reacting — paying peak rates for systems that may not be operating efficiently, and competing for service technicians who are booked solid. The window to get ahead of all of that is now, in the weeks before cooling season ramps.
