Every public and PE-backed multi-site operator names its material risk factors—the exposures significant enough to affect earnings and worth flagging to the board and to investors. For a growing number of these businesses, energy belongs on that list. It usually isn’t there yet, and in 2026 that omission has become harder to defend.
